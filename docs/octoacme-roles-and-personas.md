# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness across teams. They ensure deployment plans, approvals, and communications are aligned before launch.

### Responsibilities
- Define release scope, schedule, and go/no-go criteria
- Coordinate release checklists, dependencies, and readiness reviews
- Confirm rollback plans and release communications are prepared
- Track release risks and escalate blockers quickly

### Goals
- Deliver predictable, low-risk releases
- Minimize release-related incidents and delays
- Improve release transparency for teams and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- With Developers on build readiness, deployment steps, and rollback plans
- With Product Managers on release scope and customer-facing timing
- With Project Managers on cross-team dependencies and risk escalation
- With Stakeholders on release status, launch windows, and post-release outcomes

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear, testable requirements. They reduce ambiguity by connecting stakeholder goals to delivery details.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document requirements, constraints, and assumptions
- Refine acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers
- Support backlog readiness with clear business context
- Validate delivered behavior against expected outcomes

### Goals
- Improve requirement clarity and completeness
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or missing requirements
- Improve alignment between business intent and delivered features

### Typical Communication
- With Stakeholders to gather needs, priorities, and policy constraints
- With Product Managers to shape user stories and acceptance criteria
- With Developers to clarify edge cases and expected behavior
- With Project Managers to align requirement changes with timelines and risk

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects guide solution design and technical direction. They help teams make scalable, maintainable, and secure implementation decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and review architecture, integration patterns, and technical standards
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and design risks
- Support estimation and sequencing with engineering input
- Mentor Developers and resolve complex implementation trade-offs

### Goals
- Maintain technical quality and system reliability
- Reduce architectural risk and long-term maintenance cost
- Keep implementation decisions aligned with product and delivery goals

### Typical Communication
- With Developers on design decisions, implementation patterns, and code quality
- With Product Managers on trade-offs between scope, complexity, and value
- With Project Managers on technical risk, dependencies, and delivery feasibility
- With Stakeholders on technical implications that affect timelines or outcomes

---

## Scrum Masters / Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Delivery Leads facilitate team flow and delivery cadence. They remove blockers and improve collaboration across planning and execution.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Track blockers and drive timely resolution
- Improve team delivery practices and workflow predictability
- Protect focus by reducing process friction and context switching

### Goals
- Improve team throughput and delivery consistency
- Reduce blockers and cycle time
- Strengthen continuous improvement and team accountability

### Typical Communication
- With Developers on blockers, capacity, and sprint commitments
- With Product Managers on backlog readiness and scope adjustments
- With Project Managers on delivery status, risks, and dependency follow-through
- With Stakeholders on delivery expectations and milestone confidence

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support / Operations Representatives bring production and customer-impact context into delivery decisions. They help teams plan for operability before and after release.

### Responsibilities
- Provide operational readiness input during planning and release reviews
- Surface recurring support issues and incident patterns
- Validate monitoring, alerting, and runbook readiness
- Support incident response and post-incident improvement actions

### Goals
- Improve service stability and support readiness
- Reduce customer-impacting incidents and resolution time
- Ensure operational feedback informs future planning

### Typical Communication
- With Developers on observability gaps, incident patterns, and runbook quality
- With Product Managers on customer impact trends and support priorities
- With Project Managers on operational risk, readiness dependencies, and response plans
- With Stakeholders on service health, incident outcomes, and improvement actions
